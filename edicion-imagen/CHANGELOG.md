## [Unreleased]

## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-18

### Added

- French and Brazilian Portuguese, like LibreShard 0.5.2: the tablet's texts and the shard's
  settings. Translated by AI and not reviewed by a native speaker yet; fixes are welcome.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-18

For LibreShard 0.5.1. The buttons, keys and settings are the same.

### Changed

- It moved out of LibreShard into the gallery: LibreShard 0.5.2 no longer includes it and offers to
  install it from there. Its id is still `edicion-imagen`, so your settings, favorite and place in
  the menu stay.

### Fixed

- The help in Spanish named the shard "Edición de imagen", but LibreShard's Shards tab shows it as
  "Image editing".

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

Included with LibreShard from 0.1.0 to 0.5.1.
